# Audio Files Dropzone

Place your generated ElevenLabs voiceover audio files in this directory:

### Voiceover Audio Files Expected:
- `voice-en.wav` (English voiceover, 40 seconds)
- `voice-hi.wav` (Hindi voiceover, 40 seconds)
- `voice-te.wav` (Telugu voiceover, 40 seconds)

*(Note: MP3 format is also supported if named `voice-en.mp3`, `voice-hi.mp3`, `voice-te.mp3`)*

### Background Music Track:
- `music.wav` (Modern synth atmospheric pulse track, mixed at ~35% volume with automatic voice ducking)
